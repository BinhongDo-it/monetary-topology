## What C8-1c does not decide

- **It does not identify the contractual payment.** The floor estimates the level payment the borrower habitually makes. A borrower who rounds every payment up to the same larger number is indistinguishable here. Separating the two needs the origination fields and is **C8-1d**, registered and not run.
- **It does not compute `omega`.** It decides which payment `b8_omega.py` carries as state.
- A segment boundary is a change in field 9. Two modifications landing on the same rate with no intervening rate change would merge into one segment and bias that segment's floor downward. The count of such cases is not separated here.
- It reads no prediction, so no result here is quotable as a finding about the economy.
