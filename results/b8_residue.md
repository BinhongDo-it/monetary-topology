`flat` is `|B(t) - B(t-1)|` within 1e-07 of the balance, whose source is B8-0b's floor (half a cent over a median balance of 165,000 is 3.03e-08). `sched` is within 0.0001 of `B(t-1)*(1+i/1200) - P`. **`other` is neither, and if it carries the gap the reading is wrong and something else is happening.**
