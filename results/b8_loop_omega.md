## 3. Is the assembly consistent

**Two checks, and the second one is the real one.** `identity` is §17.11's registered assertion. Under a prefix-sum implementation the three legs telescope to the loop sum **whatever `t_M` is**, so it cannot catch the misplaced vertex §17.11 says it catches. `replay` re-sums a sample of loops month by month from the window indices and can.

Tolerance `3.730e-12`, derived in the module docstring. A misaligned window moves a sum by one month's residual, of order 1e-3, **nine orders away**.

## What this does not decide

- **It reads no prediction.** B8-1, B8-2 and B8-3 all judge against B8-0b's floor, which is not computed on loops here.
- **The leg split is bookkeeping** (§14.2). Arrears capitalisation lands in leg 2 rather than leg 1 and the loop sum does not care.
- **`omega3` is not evidence about HAMP trial periods.** §14.3 as amended: the multi-onset carrier is 38 loops across six archives.
- The loans carrying both zero-interest balances are **refused, not estimated** (C13). Their count is in section 1 and travels with every figure above.
- §7's four filters (single family, first lien, owner occupied, fixed rate) are **still not applied**.
