# O35: contract moves with no flag on B8-0b's floor arm

Generated by `experiments/b8_o35_floor_steps.py`. Disposition of O35 in the project's objection cache. The criteria are written in the script that produces this record, the record carries their text, and every quantity this run produces is reported.

**What O35 was.** `b8_2_curve.md` section 4 recomputed the clean-cure arm under the far-corner curve construction. Five archives moved by 1.8e-12 to 1.5e-11. **2017Q1 moved by 3.102e-06, 115 times its floor**, on an arm where the argument printed above that table says the annuity factor cancels whatever the curve is.

**The identity.** With no zero-interest balance `V(b, i, n, d) = b * V(1, i, n, d)`, so each month of a loop sum splits exactly into the ideal part and a contract term:

```
r(t) = log( B(t) / B_hat(t) )  +  C(t)
C(t) = log V(1, i(t), n(t), d(t))  -  log V(1, i(t-1), n(t-1) - 1, d(t))
```

`i` is field 9, `n` field 17, `d` the curve at the row's month and horizon. **`C(t)` is zero when the term steps down by one month and the note rate holds.** A term step makes it depend on `d`, which is what a change of curve can see. **A rate step leaves `d` out of it entirely**, so the test that found O35 cannot see that half, and this file looks for both.


**The reading.** Of 403,090 loops in B8-0b's population over 6 archives, **3 hold a step**: 2 a change of note rate and 1 a change of term, and none carries a flag in field 42, 63 or 108. Removing each one's contract term leaves it inside B8-0a's own rounding bound, and the contract term on every other loop in the population is exactly zero. **O35's figure is the term-step loop** (2017Q1, loan 573498944173), read back as 3.102e-06. The floor's `MAD` moves in its fifth significant figure only (2002Q1: 5.2162e-08 to 5.2161e-08), and the range B8-0b's verdict quotes at three figures reads the same. **The variance-type readings move by orders of magnitude**: with the named loops out, `sqrt(Z)/sqrt(N)` reads 2.7e+05 to 2.1e+06 on every arm of every archive (2002Q1 from 7.06e+02 to 8.33e+05 pooled; 2017Q1 from 1.51e+04 to 1.46e+06 pooled), and `2*Var` on the floor settles with the sample size, the widest spread at the largest proper subsample being 4.4x (2002Q1, n = 30,000). Across whole archives, the term steps with no flag are concentrated in **2020-04** on 5 of 6 archives.
