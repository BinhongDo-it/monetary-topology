## What this does not decide

- **It makes no causal claim.** B8-3 is an existence claim about the state space (§15.7, §19.1). Section 2 is stronger than B8-3 needs and section 3 tests only whether the arm label carries information given the path and the window.
- **Section 2 cannot repair the measurability difference** between the arms; a loop with no `omega` is in no cell.
- §5's secondary timing pairs are **registered and not run** (§19.2).
- B8-1 and B8-2 have their own criteria and are not read here.
