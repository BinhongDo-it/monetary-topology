## What this does not decide

- **It does not name the mechanism.** A balance that freezes and catches up is consistent with a posting cut-off, with a servicer reporting the prior month's figure, and with other things. The shape is what is measured.
- **It does not decide the B8-0a(i) tolerance**, it supplies the counts.
- The recovery tolerance in §2 is a construction choice and is stated with the table rather than buried.
- Recovery is looked for **inside a segment only**, so a freeze in the month before a modification is censored by construction. §4 counts those.
- It reads no prediction, so no result here is quotable as a finding about the economy.
