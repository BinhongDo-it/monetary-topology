# B8: is the curve-construction choice load bearing?

Generated by `experiments/b8_cmt_sensitivity.py`. Registered in `docs/b8_fannie_slice.md` §16.11.

**This measures whether the choice matters. It does not make the choice.**

Rules crossed: interpolation ['linear_in_tenor', 'linear_in_log_tenor'], beyond the longest tenor ['cap', 'linear_in_tenor', 'linear_in_log_tenor'], so 6 constructions per modification. The reported quantity is the **spread of `log V` across all of them**, which is what reaches `r`; a construction that shifts every horizon equally cancels in `r` and is not counted here.

**Reads no prediction.**

## What this does not decide

- **It does not choose either rule.** It says whether the choice can be seen through the measurement.
- **It does not price a single loop.** `V` here is a level annuity on the modification row, which is the horizon the rule acts on, not the full `omega` construction.
- **It says nothing about the 47-month gap's own rule.** In those months the longest tenor is the 20-year and the extrapolation distance is longer; §3 counts them and they are pooled above, not isolated.
- It reads no prediction.
