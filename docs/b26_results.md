# B26 results: the headline number held while three of its four fields moved

Criteria fixed in [`b26_prereg.md`](b26_prereg.md). Sources and their calibers in
[`b26_focal_price_availability.md`](b26_focal_price_availability.md).

**All four arms read. The station is not void.**

## Readings

| arm | verdict | detail |
|---|---|---|
| **B26-1** headline scalar | **third state** | the announced number never changed. It was preserved while its scope was emptied |
| **B26-2** margin count | **three of four moved** | drinks, stores and frequency all moved; the price field did not |
| **B26-3** shock magnitude | **passes, with room** | unit cost `+35.9%` to `+46.2%` against a `20%` threshold |
| **B26-4** positive control | **passes** | non-focal posted prices moved in the same firm and window |

## B26-1. The number was preserved and the offer was hollowed out

`9.9` was announced nationally in June 2023. In August 2023, at the second-quarter results
call, the chairman stated it would be normalised and continue for at least two
years. **Original:** *"当年6月开始的9.9元感恩回馈活动反响超预期，因此决定将这一
优惠活动常态化进行下去，至少持续两年。"* (郭谨一, 瑞幸咖啡董事长兼CEO). It was never
changed to another number. What changed was everything around it, and by 2025 the
named zone had gone, replaced by a "from `9.9`" framing that keeps the digits and
drops the commitment.

**The third state was preregistered as a distinct prediction rather than a
fallback, and it is what occurred.** A firm under this cost pressure had an
ordinary alternative available: move the number. It did not.

**Independently reproduced at a second firm, which was not part of the design.**
The competitor ended its own all-items `9.9` from February 2026 by the same
route: **the number was kept for a special zone of three to seven products while
everything else reverted to regular prices of `11.9` to `16.9`.** At the time of
the report a single product carried `9.9` and it was out of stock. On the first
firm's side most products had returned to `10.9` to `13.9`.

**Two sellers, same market, same shock, and neither changed the number.** Both
withdrew the offer by narrowing what it covered.

## B26-2. Three of four fields moved

The four fields are the seller's own, fixed in the design before reading.

| field | movement |
|---|---|
| **price** | **none.** `9.9` throughout |
| eligible drinks | all freshly made coffee (2023-06) → ten named (2024-02) → **six named (2024-04)** → eight (early 2025) → zone removed → a few basic drinks |
| participating stores | **moved, and stated officially.** On 2024-04-22 customer service, original: *"之前是天天券，指的是部分门店可以参与，现在是全国券，它是两种不同的券。"* and *"活动内容有所更改，之前的天天券已升级为全国门店可使用，目前全国券只能选择6款饮品，具体以使用规则为准。"* |
| frequency | daily → one coupon per week → the standing zone replaced by an afternoon-tea slot |

**The store field moved outward while the drink field moved inward, in the same
announcement.** The offer was being reshaped on two axes at once with the number
fixed, which is a sharper observation than any single field moving.

## B26-3. The shock was large enough that not moving is a reading

Arabica futures rose about `70%` over 2024; the C-type contract rose close to
`90%` over six months and reached `429.95` US cents per pound. A published unit
cost decomposition for a comparable operator in the same market puts raw material
at `5.7` of `11.1` yuan, a share of `0.5135`.

```
unit cost increase  =  0.70 x 0.5135  =  +35.9%
                       0.90 x 0.5135  =  +46.2%
```

Against a threshold of `20%` fixed before the reading. **A cost passthrough
account predicts the price field moves. It did not.**

**Caliber note, and it constrains what this arm licenses.** The raw material share
is taken from a different firm's published decomposition and is used **only as a
share, never as a level**. The reading is that the shock is large relative to unit
cost in this business, not that any particular seller's unit cost took a
particular value.

## B26-4. The seller's price system is not globally rigid

Non-focal posted prices at the same seller moved over the same window. Two
individual items are reported at `29` to `32` and `11` to `16`, and the general
reversion of most of the range to `10.9` to `13.9` is reported separately.

**So rigidity is a property of that number, not of this seller's prices in
general.** Had nothing moved, the design voids the other three arms, and that
outcome was available.

**Caliber note.** The two individual item moves are the weakest source used in
this file, a single trade report, and one of the two is difficult to reconcile
with a broker report giving that drink a list price of `18` in 2023. **The arm
does not rest on them**: the range-wide reversion is separately reported and is
what carries the count.

## Placement

**Against a cost passthrough account: distinguishing.** B26-1 with B26-3 is the
pair that does it. A `36` to `46` per cent unit cost increase with no change to
the price field is not what that account predicts.

**Against short-run price stickiness: separated by B26-4.** Other posted prices at
the same seller moved in the same window, so the constancy is not general
stickiness.

**A quantity nobody else assembles: B26-2's four-field count.** No competing
account has a reason to enumerate the fields of an offer and ask which of them
absorbed the adjustment.

## Independent confirmation, and what it is worth

**Neither of these readings was advanced as a discovery.** They establish a
premise. The payload of the framework section they serve is a separate claim,
stated there as a hypothesis and untouched by anything measured here.

**The premise turns out to be established in the literature, on other carriers,
by tighter designs than either station could run.**

- **Uniform pricing despite local demand.** DellaVigna, S. and Gentzkow, M.
  (2019), *Uniform Pricing in U.S. Retail Chains*, Quarterly Journal of Economics
  134(4), 2011-2084. US food, drugstore and mass-merchandise chains charge
  nearly uniform prices across stores despite wide variation in consumer
  demographics and competition; the median chain forgoes about **`16` million
  dollars a year** relative to store-level optimal pricing. **Their design is
  tighter than this project's**: same chain, same country, same regulation, so
  cost is not merely converged but shared. They also record that uniform pricing
  raises what poorer households pay relative to richer ones.
- **Rigidity at focal price points.** Snir, A., Levy, D., Gotler, A. and Chen, H.
  find that nine-ending prices are more common and more rigid than others, that
  consumers treat the ending as a cheapness signal and are **less likely to
  notice a larger price ending in nine**, and that the rigidity is asymmetric,
  holding upward and not downward. See also Levy, Lee, Chen, Kauffman and Bergen,
  *Price Points and Price Rigidity*.

**Three unrelated carriers, three unrelated methods, one phenomenon.** US retail
chains, cross-country consumer electronics, and Chinese prepared coffee. That
convergence is worth more than any of the three alone and it is the reason to
cite rather than to compete.

**What the literature does not do, and this is the whole of what remains.** The
uniform pricing result is presented there as a puzzle and explained by managerial
simplicity, inattention and fairness concerns. **No claim is made that the
structure generates an obstruction.** The step from "posted prices are
conventional and rigid" to "convention propagated around a cycle admits no global
potential" is not taken in that literature, is not taken here, and is the only
part still unclaimed.

**Citation status.** Both records were read from abstracts and working paper
pages. **Neither full text has been read**, and page-level claims must be checked
against the version of record before use.

## What this does not establish

**Does not establish** that individual menu items were or were not at the focal
number on any given date. No unified menu state exists to read and the design
excludes the claim.

**Does not establish** anything about the cost shock's own effect. There is one
shock in the window; identification is across margins within it, and a claim about
the shock as a treatment would need shocks it does not have.

**Does not establish** that the number is rigid in general or at other sellers.
Two sellers behaved the same way, one of them outside the design, which is
suggestive and is reported as suggestive.

**Does not establish** a mechanism for why the number is the rigid object. The
station reads that it was rigid and that three other fields were not.
