# What this claims, and which stations are in your field

**This is the overview and the index. It is not a volume.** The volumes are
numbered after the manuscript they implement; this file sits above them.

The first half states what the programme claims and why the claim is one claim
rather than a pile of stations. The second half is an index by field.

**The volumes themselves are ordered by station number**, which is an internal
build order and tells an outside reader nothing. Economics is not one audience,
and the object being measured here is unfamiliar in most of its rooms, so a
reader who opens Volume II at B7 has no way to know whether B7 is in their field
or three fields away.

Each section below names the stations that sit in one field, states what they
found **in that field's own vocabulary**, names the nearest thing the field
already does, and says how this differs from it. **Read your own section, then
go to those stations.** Nobody needs to read all of it.

## Why this is one programme and not a pile of stations

Most of economics is built on one assumption that is almost never written down as
an assumption: **that the terms on which things exchange are generated by a price
field.** One number attached to each good, each position, each date. Everyone
faces the same number, and everything that looks like a departure from it —
spreads, wedges, premia, discrimination, frictions — is modelled as an adjustment
*to* that number rather than as evidence that no such number exists.

The assumption does an enormous amount of work, and most of it is invisible.
Aggregation rests on it: a price index is only meaningful if there is a field to
index. The welfare theorems rest on it: a competitive equilibrium is defined by
one price vector all agents face. Comparative statics rests on it. So does the
practice of estimating a "the" effect of a policy on "the" price of something.
None of these fail gracefully if the assumption is false. They stop being
defined.

**The assumption is testable, and the test has an exact zero.**

If the terms are generated by a scalar field, then walking any closed cycle of
positions and adding the costs with sign gives zero. Not approximately zero, not
zero up to a residual, not small relative to a benchmark. **Zero, as an
arithmetic identity.** Additive separability, path independence, and
cycle-sums-vanish are three names for the same property. This is old mathematics
and it is not in dispute; the contribution here is noticing that it converts a
foundational assumption into something you can go and measure on administrative
data, and then going and measuring it.

**On four independent carriers it is not zero.**

Mortgage servicing on 170 million monthly rows. ETF creation and redemption on
6,201 fund-days. Cross-currency funding on 1.5 million rows. A second GSE. These
are different institutions, different regulators, different data vendors,
different decades, and different failure modes. They are not four tests of one
dataset. **Each one is a carrier of the same object**, and the object is the same
because the cycle sum is defined by the graph and not by the setting.

**What follows is narrow, and the narrowness is the point.** If the loop sum
carries a non-trivial class, then no global scalar potential exists for those
terms, and therefore no objective can be globally realised through the price
mechanism by that coordination architecture. That is the whole claim. It requires
**one inequality on one cycle**. It requires no utility function, no
interpersonal comparison, no welfare criterion, and no assertion that the number
is large. A reader who wants to reject it has to attack the measurement, not the
philosophy, and the stations are organised to make that attack cheap.

**The obvious objection is that 1.7 basis points is nothing, and it misses the
form of the claim.** The claim is not that the number is big. It is that the
number is **not zero**, and that property is binary in a way magnitudes are not:
a field either admits a global potential or it does not, and one non-vanishing
cycle settles it. The magnitude question is a different and later question about
how much the failure costs, which this programme does not answer and does not
claim to.

**What it would reorganise if it holds.** Within-cell rate dispersion in
mortgages, covered-interest-parity deviations, ETF arbitrage residuals, parallel
exchange rate gaps, and path dependence in loan modification are currently five
literatures with five sets of frictions and no reason to talk to each other. On
this reading they are **five carriers of one measurement**, and the frictions are
not five explanations but five places the same structure becomes visible. That is
a strong claim and it is the reason the carriers were deliberately chosen to be
institutionally unrelated: shared structure across unrelated settings is the only
evidence that would distinguish one object from five coincidences.

**The honest counterweight, stated here rather than buried.** One station proves
a result that **weakens** this framework: when conversions run one way only, a
non-zero directed loop sum no longer implies an obstruction. Another shows the
question **dissolves entirely** on a single-index price vector, so it is only
real for a genuinely two-index field. The unifying claim on the mechanism side
has an empirical form that **has never been run**. And roughly half of what this
programme produced is a failure, recorded at the same resolution as the passes.

**None of that is hedging.** It is the reason the positive readings are worth
anything: a programme that could not have produced these three admissions would
not have been able to produce a real zero either.

## The one paragraph that everything else rests on

The quantity measured throughout is a **closed-loop sum**. Take a set of
positions an agent can occupy, and a rule that assigns a cost to moving between
two of them. Walk a cycle that returns to where it started and add the costs
with sign. If the terms of exchange are generated by one consistent scalar price
attached to each position, that sum is exactly zero, always, on every cycle.
Additive separability and path independence are the same statement.

So a persistently non-zero cycle sum is not dispersion, not noise, and not an
interpretation. **It is a measurement that no single price field generates the
observed terms.** The technical name is non-integrability, and the object is a
cohomology class. That is the entire import of the topology: it supplies a
falsifiable zero, and everything downstream is an argument about whether a
measured non-zero clears the instrument.

**What this does not claim** is set in B0 and is narrower than readers expect. It
needs one inequality on one cycle. It needs no interpersonal comparison, no
welfare function, and no statement about whether the non-zero is large.

## Market microstructure and asset pricing

**Stations: B9, B14, B3.**

**B9** measures the loop on ETF creation and redemption, where all three
positions (shares, the creation basket, cash) are institutional objects with
published prices, and where nobody has to choose a discretisation. Sixteen funds,
404 trading days, 6,201 fund-days of closing NBBO midpoint. The loop sum is
**1.2 to 1.7 basis points**, standing at **1.05 to 5.08 times the measurement
floor**. The tick-quantisation explanation is ruled out by measurement rather
than by argument: tick size varies across the sample by a factor of 15.8 while
the loop sum varies by 1.42.

**B14** is the identification station. Section 5.1 of the directed-graph result
left an invariance claim untested and named exactly what a test would need: a
**dated, exogenous friction change that hits both agent classes equally**. The
2016 tick size pilot is that. The gate passes on the pilot's start, six of six.
**The mirror test on the pilot's end returns three of six**, and the
pre-registered reading of that outcome was followed rather than argued with, so
the earlier round is now under question by the station's own rule.

**B3** reads cross-currency funding on the Du–Keerati–Schreger CIP dataset,
1,513,471 rows, 2000 to 2025, and asks whether cycles that never touch the US
Treasury vanish, which is what a single common convenience yield would require.

**Nearest existing practice.** Limits-to-arbitrage, and the ETF arbitrage and CIP
deviation literatures. Those measure a **deviation from a benchmark price**: how
far did this price get from where no-arbitrage says it should be.

**How this differs.** A cycle sum needs no benchmark and no model of the correct
price. It is a self-contained arithmetic identity on observed quantities, and its
null is exactly zero rather than an estimated fair value. That buys a sharper
zero and gives up the ability to say the deviation is *large*: a cycle sum is a
statement that no consistent price field exists, not a statement about how much
money is on the table.

**What is still open.** B9's ceiling is measurement precision on published
closing quotes, and the station says so. B14's mirror result is live and
unresolved.

## Household finance and consumer credit

**Stations: B8, B10, B2, B7.**

**B8 is the strongest single reading in the programme.** One mortgage at a fixed
position, with servicing states `current`, `delinquent`, `modified` and
`deferred`. The cost on an edge is the change in the present value of the
household's remaining contractual obligation against the counterfactual in which
the contract was never re-signed. 170,013,011 monthly rows, 2,942,295 loans, six
acquisition quarters, 85,308 closed loops. The finding: **two realisable routes to
the same servicing state carry different accumulated cost, in six of six
cohorts**, five to six orders of magnitude above an instrument constant that a
zero-parameter prediction reproduces to within 15%, with a permutation null
returning 0 of 999 in all six.

In the field's own terms: **the terms a household ends up with depend on the path
it walked to get there, and not only on where it is now.** Two borrowers
identically situated today, one of whom reached that state through modification
and one directly, do not hold contractually equivalent obligations.

**B10** replicates the design on Freddie Mac (1,362,490 loans, 74,937,616 monthly
rows, 28 vintages, 17,875 closed triangles) and contributes a structural result
that is cheap to state and independently interesting: **of 10,816 possible
ordered state pairs, only 1,496 ever occur, 13.83%.** The servicing state graph
is far sparser than the state space implies, which bounds what any path-dependence
design on this data can reach.

**B2 and B7** are the origination cross-section, on HMDA modified LAR, 20,071,900
reported spreads. B2 asks whether two applicants for materially the same
financing, at the same quarter, tract, tier, occupancy, lien and purpose, receive
materially different terms; within-cell share of rate-spread variance is
**0.7831** unrestricted and **0.8480** at a minimum cell size of 20. B7 asks how
many independent directions the local deviation from the national table needs.

**Nearest existing practice.** The pricing-dispersion and discrimination
literature on originations, and the loan-modification literature on servicing
outcomes.

**How this differs.** Both of those are cross-sectional: they compare borrowers
at a point in time. B8's object is **within one loan, across time**, and the
comparison is between two routes rather than between two people, which removes
the borrower-heterogeneity problem that the cross-sectional literature spends
most of its effort on.

**What is still open.** B7's first estimator read rank 2 and **that reading was
withdrawn**; a second estimator was built after the station closed. The
withdrawal is in the station's own text.

## International finance and exchange rate regimes

**Stations: B5, B6, B15, B3.**

These are the carriers where **the eligibility rule is written in regulation
rather than inferred**, which is unusual and is the reason they were chosen. When
a government publishes several legal prices for the same conversion and states in
law who may use which, the agent index is not a latent variable.

**B5, Argentina.** Three series, 5,248 rows, and a dated intervention: on
14 April 2025 the cepo was removed and the USD 200 monthly cap deleted. The
treated pair collapses when the government deletes the eligibility rule that
generates it. **The station's pre-trend check then returned no reading, and the
band that had first made that a failure was withdrawn** for having no
theoretical source. What the arm prints is the pre-window series, which turns
inside the window and so admits no slope that can be extrapolated across the
edge.

**B6, Cuba.** The central bank prices `CUP ↔ USD` three ways on the same day: a
frozen enterprise rate, a frozen retail rate, and a managed float opened
2025-12-18. The first two admit no return leg, so a position can be entered and
not left. This is the first carrier to give the `H⁰` half of the object a positive
reading, which is a different structure from the loop sums everywhere else.

**B15, Bolivia.** A regulated multiple-rate regime with the operative documents
read from source. The station is open: one criterion is void and the third arm is
suspended on it.

**Nearest existing practice.** The parallel-exchange-rate and capital-control
literatures, and the CIP deviation literature for B3.

**How this differs.** Those literatures typically treat the gap between official
and parallel rates as the object. Here the object is whether **a cycle through
several legal prices closes**, which is a different quantity and can be non-zero
even when every individual gap is small. The regulated-eligibility setting also
supplies something the rest of the programme lacks: a **deletion event**, where
the rule generating the structure is removed on a known date by an actor with no
interest in this measurement.

## Corporate finance and credit

**Station: B11.**

The design asks whether the mortgage ring replicates in a second domain:
`investment grade → downgrade → distressed exchange → re-rating`, where the
distressed exchange is the counterpart of a mortgage modification because that is
where the contract is re-signed.

**The domain was not chosen. It was fixed in advance by a branch table** written
before the mortgage results were known, which is what stops the second domain
from being selected to match the first.

**Status: gated and not closed.** Availability is established and the markers are
measured; the domain's own gate is not yet computed. The ceiling is arithmetic:
227 issuers carry the required marker in the available window, and whether that
is enough is the gate.

**Nearest existing practice.** The distressed exchange and rating migration
literatures.

**How this differs.** Those measure outcomes conditional on a transition. This
measures whether **the composition of transitions around a closed ring** fails to
cancel, which is a statement about the pricing of the path rather than about the
transition.

## Macroeconomics, inequality, and monetary policy

**Stations: the whole A track, plus the empirical fingerprints arm.**

The A track asks one question: if claims keep accumulating at the top of a
stratified payment network, **does the bottom get cut off from real resources
even while total spending and total transaction volume are flat or rising?**

The readings that matter to this field:

- **A0.** Raising the top holder's spending rate from 0 to 30 per round, with the
  adjacency matrix held fixed, delivers no additional resources to the bottom
  forty. The registered criterion is that the slope be statistically
  indistinguishable from zero, and it is. **Spending inside your own stratum is
  functionally equivalent to saving.**
- **A2.** With no issuance, flow and reach move together. **With issuance they move
  opposite**: flow rises 44.87 times while reach contracts. This is the track's
  strongest reading and it is the one that says aggregate volume is not
  informative about who is reached.
- **A2c.** Gross flow rises 97.1 times while net displacement stays flat within
  1.62 times. **Cycle rank collapses to 0.029 of potential with no edge deleted.**
- **A6.** Under already-fair retention, the stratified graph needs a
  redistribution rate of **0.060** to match the flat graph, which needs **0.000**.
  Six tax points, and that difference is the cost of the structure, stated in
  units of tax.
- **A4 and A7.** The four standard explanations for wealth divergence are
  inheritance, education returns, heterogeneous capital returns, and assortative
  mating. A4 asks whether connectivity is a fifth item on that list or the
  variable that sets the space the other four operate in. A7 makes connectivity
  continuous rather than a switch, and **partly undercuts A3**, which the volume
  says in its own heading.
- **A1** is the only A-track stage attached to real household data.

**Nearest existing practice.** The inequality-decomposition literature, and
heterogeneous-agent models where the distribution is a state variable.

**How this differs.** In those, network position is either absent or a reduced-form
parameter. Here it is the primitive, and the four standard explanations are treated
as operating **inside** a space that connectivity fixes, rather than as competing
additive channels.

**The honest ceiling, stated by the track itself.** The coverage test — one
parameter vector producing all four surfaces at once — **has never run**. Until
it does, the unified-mechanism claim has no empirical content in this
implementation, and the criterion's form has separately been ruled defective from
the other track's side because it lists what the framework explains and never
what it forbids.

## Econometrics, identification, and measurement

**Stations: B1, B4, B12, B13, B7.**

This is the section for readers who want to attack the design rather than the
subject. **Two of these stations weaken the framework's own claims**, which is why
they are listed here rather than buried.

**B1** asks whether the whole question dissolves into notation. It does on one
index and survives on two: on a single-currency price vector, `log R_ij` is
exactly a gradient, so **integrability is a theorem of notation there**, and the
question is only real for a genuinely two-index field.

**B4 runs against the framework.** When a conversion runs one way only, a non-zero
directed loop sum does not imply an obstruction. A sub-potential exists if and only
if no directed cycle sums positive, which is strictly weaker than exactness.
**Directedness helps the null**, and the station says so plainly.

**B12** exists because of an objection. A previous ruler could not measure what was
asked of it, so B12 re-asks the question with **a ruler whose prediction is an
exact zero**, which is the property that makes a test able to fail. Pre-registered,
code written, and every record is still marked diagnostic.

**B13** answers the strongest methodological objection in the programme: coverage
tests are worth nothing because they list what a framework explains and never what
it forbids. The repair is a **zero domain** — the framework must first say where
its own quantity is required to be zero, then go and measure there. **Closed in a
day, on a vendor's free public sample, with no money spent.**

**B7** is where the interactive-fixed-effects reader should go, and it carries a
limit that must travel with it: **a rank of 2 does not mean standard econometrics
cannot reach this.** Interactive fixed effects are exactly a two-way interaction
of r factors. The substantive claim is about the mortgage market, not about
irreducibility.

**Nearest existing practice.** Interactive fixed effects for B7; pre-registration
and specification-curve practice for the design side.

**How this differs.** The design side's own finding is negative and is recorded as
such: **declared-in-advance readings caught defects, and threshold gates did not.**
Where a criterion drew a line on an estimate, it either wasted the run or was
itself broken; where a criterion forced an object to be printed, it earned its
cost. That is a result about method, obtained the expensive way.

## Networks, complex systems, and agent-based modelling

**Stations: A0, A0b, A2, A2c, A3, A5, A7.**

**A0b** finds a threshold rather than a gradient: with a wage bill that responds
to derived demand, **unit elasticity is the dividing line.** Below it a positive
steady state exists; at or above it there is none, and above the line survival is
strictly linear in the autonomous share.

**A3** is the hinge between the two tracks, and it is the one number that connects
a full simulated economy to a measurement module. A divergence exponent produced
by the simulation and a loop sum produced by a module **given nothing but a terms
matrix and two price vectors** agree to 3.05%, with the absence of shared code
enforced mechanically so the agreement cannot be an artefact of shared
implementation.

**A5** finds the two layers separated by a factor of about 23 in effective
reachability at A3's registered parameters, and **four of its eight criteria fail
and are not fixed**; all four were entered into an expected-failure list with
reasons, before the fact.

**Nearest existing practice.** The standard ABM validation checklist: endogenous
cycles, fat tails, pro- and counter-cyclicality, volatility ordering, right-skewed
distributions, power laws, counter-cyclical labour share, endogenous financial
crises.

**How this differs, and it is a deliberate refusal.** That checklist is **not used
as a criterion here.** Dozens of existing models reproduce most of it, including
models with no stratified structure at all, so reproducing it buys no
distinguishing credit; and the list is a list of aggregate moments, while this
framework's own claim is that aggregate readings are projections that lose the
structure in question. Scoring well on an instrument you have argued is blind is
not evidence. The checklist belongs in an appendix, and it is treated as one.

**The distinguishing outputs instead** are the ones nobody has had a reason to
measure: the size of the reached set moving opposite to total volume; a structural
component whose magnitude stays flat while its share collapses; the fraction of a
field that is integrable.

## What is not here

- **The coverage test has not run.** See the macro section.
- **Two stations are open for want of data**, not for want of design, and both
  produced results worth having before they stopped.
- **B4 weakens the framework** in the directed setting, and is listed in the
  econometrics section rather than omitted.
- **B7's first estimator was withdrawn**, and the withdrawal is in the text.
- **B5's pre-trend check is void and the threshold behind it was withdrawn**, in
  the text and not in a footnote: an arbitrary calibration value may not ground a
  negative finding, and undecidable is not the same state as decided-against.
- **Every failed criterion is in [RESULTS.md](../RESULTS.md)** with its reading, on
  the same terms as the passing ones. A failed criterion is citable.

**How to cite anything here**: by criterion identifier, such as `B8-3` or `A5-6`,
never by section number. Section numbers move and identifiers do not. Citing a
passing criterion without its scope limits is a misreading of it.
